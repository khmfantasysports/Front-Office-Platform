KHM Fantasy Sports

Built for the front-office side of fantasy sports.

RosterCap is an independent KHM Fantasy Sports product focused on giving fantasy managers a configurable workspace for the roster-management work that traditional league platforms often leave scattered across spreadsheets and notes.

RosterCap

RosterCap brings roster organization, positional depth, contracts, salary-cap or budget tracking, development rosters, future assets and transaction history into one private Front Office.

Configuration first Each Front Office saves its own league structure. Sport presets provide sensible terminology and position choices without forcing one league model on every user.
Multi-sport RosterCap supports fantasy workflows across NHL, NFL, NBA and MLB, including sport-aware Fantrax CSV import mapping.
Private workspace Front Office data is tied to the authenticated account and protected by application and database access controls.
Transaction consequences RosterCap is designed so roster, contract, cap and asset consequences can flow from the transaction that created them instead of requiring duplicate bookkeeping.

KHM Fantasy Sports

KHM Fantasy Sports is the independent fantasy-sports product brand behind RosterCap. Its focus is practical software and data tools for fantasy managers who want more control over roster construction, contracts, long-term assets and league-specific rules.

RosterCap is not affiliated with or endorsed by the professional sports leagues, teams or fantasy platforms referenced within the product.

Current product status

RosterCap is actively developed. Features may continue to evolve as the platform is tested across different league structures and sports. User-configured league settings remain the source of truth for each Front Office.
